# Working Together

A one-page-per-screen tool for speech pathologists to get to know a family, agree how you'll work together, and set and track shared goals. Built for initial assessments and ongoing check-ins in paediatric speech pathology.

**Open it:** https://ppspeech.github.io/working-together/

## What's in it

1. **Getting to know you** – what brings the family in, hopes, family and culture, languages at home, the child, the family's week, who else is involved, plus a background row (diagnoses, health and hearing, development, feeding and sleep, kindy/school, previous therapy).
2. **Working together** – what happens in the service, contact, appointments, everyone's roles, how the family wants to be involved, good times to practise, and the plan for checking in.
3. **Goals** – one goal per screen: the goal, the child's own words, where we are now, the long-term goal, a progress line, the plan and home activities, an optional SMARTER goal statement, and dated check-ins.
4. **Milestones** – a plain-language "is this normal?" guide by age, with visuals, for talking with parents.

Every box has a **?** with conversation prompts. Download a PDF of the whole file from any tab.

## Privacy

Client files are saved **only in the browser you use** (localStorage). Nothing is uploaded or sent anywhere. That also means files don't sync between devices, and clearing browser data deletes them, so use **Back up all clients** regularly and **Restore from backup** to move files to another device. Keep backup files somewhere secure, as they contain client information.

## Sources and acknowledgements

- Sheet structure and conversation prompts adapted from the Northern Sydney Local Health District (NSLHD) Child, Youth and Family Speech Pathology engagement toolkit ("Getting to know you and your family" and "Our goals and how we will work together"), published as supplementary material in the *International Journal of Speech-Language Pathology*. Prompts are paraphrased.
- Goal framework informed by Taliancich-Klinger & Kennedy (2023), *Goal Setting in Speech-Language Pathology: A Guide to Clinical Reasoning*, Plural Publishing, and SMARTER goals (Hersh et al., 2012).
- Child's-words prompts informed by the DECIDE framework for child-led goal setting (Ryan et al., 2024).
- Milestones paraphrased from Shipley & McAfee, *Assessment in Speech-Language Pathology*; Roth & Worthington (2019), *Treatment Resource Manual for Speech-Language Pathology*; and Boudreau & Costanza-Smith (2011), *Language, Speech, and Hearing Services in Schools*, 42. Milestones are a guide only, not a diagnosis.
